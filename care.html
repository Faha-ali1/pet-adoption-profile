PET CARE & SHELTER GUIDE

Everything you need to know to keep your adopted furry friend healthy and happy.

1. Healthy Diet

Provide fresh clean water daily and age-appropriate nutritious pet food twice a day.

2. Daily Exercise

Take dogs for a 30-minute walk daily and keep indoor toys available for active play.

3. First Week at Home

Give your new pet a quiet, cozy corner to settle in and visit a vet within the first week.
